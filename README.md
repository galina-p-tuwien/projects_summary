# Projects

This is an overview of past and current major projects. 

## SIMULTAN

This project was developed over the course of 11 years by an interdisciplinary team of software and civil engineers at the [TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/cee/mbb/bph) under the lead of Prof. Thomas Bednar.
